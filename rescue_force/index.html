About

What is ITM
Rescue Force?

Mission

To create a unified rescue system where human expertise and artificial intelligence work together to save lives in Bangladesh — responding to all natural disasters, accidents, fires, floods and health emergencies with maximum speed and precision.

Vision

A future where no one is left behind in a disaster — every human, animal and community receives world-class protection through smart technology, coordinated management and compassionate response from trained rescue professionals.

Values

Care. Safety. Honesty. Respect for all living beings. We believe technology guided by strong human values becomes a genuine force for good — and that ITM is the proof. Information Technology plus Management equals lives saved.

ITM = Information Technology + Management — Together, saving lives.

Knowledge

Quick First Aid Reference

Essential procedures every person should know. Ask RescueGPT for detailed step-by-step guides on any of these topics.

Severe Bleeding

Apply firm direct pressure with clean cloth. Elevate limb above heart. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top. Maintain pressure for 15 minutes. Call 108.

Burns

Cool under cold running water for 20 minutes. Never use ice, butter or cream. Remove jewelry near burn. Cover loosely with clean bandage. Call 108 for large burns.

Choking

Encourage forceful coughing. Give 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades. Follow with 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich). Call 999 if object remains lodged.

CPR

Call 108 first. 30 compressions at 100-120/min, 5cm deep. Then 2 rescue breaths. Continue 30:2 cycle until ambulance arrives. Compression-only CPR is also effective.

Heatstroke

Move to cool shade immediately. Apply ice packs to neck, armpits, groin. Fan continuously. If conscious, give small sips of water. Call 108 — heatstroke is life-threatening.

Snake Bite

Keep victim calm and still. Immobilize bitten limb below heart. Remove jewelry. Go to hospital within 4 hours for antivenom. Never cut, suck, or apply ice to wound.
